## Review Scope

This review covers the user survey conducted for the AI-assisted mental health application. The goal is to verify that the survey questions, participant group, results, and analysis are documented clearly and provide useful input for requirements elicitation and validation.

## Reviewed Artefacts

* User Survey
* Survey Questionnaire
* Survey Results

## Review Criteria

1. Correct artefact type and unique acronym
2. Clear survey objective
3. Appropriate participant group
4. Clear and unbiased questions
5. Complete documentation of response options
6. Traceable and understandable results
7. Appropriate handling of anonymity and sensitive data
8. Sufficient information for deriving requirements

## Issues Identified

1. The distribution description should use the official name `Google Forms`.
2. The participant-approach description contains a grammatical error and should refer to a WhatsApp message.
3. The survey contains only 11 participants from two master’s programs, so the results should not be generalized to all university students.
4. Response options and measurement scales are not documented for several questions.
5. The reversed privacy question may confuse participants because of its negative wording.
6. The analysis describes general tendencies but does not provide exact response counts or percentages.
7. The survey-result images do not contain explanatory captions or direct references from the analysis.
8. The paths to additional documents use inconsistent formatting.
9. The artefact should explain how consent, anonymity, and sensitive mental-health information were handled.
10. The history entry is commented out and should be activated.

## Review Result

The survey provides useful information about student stress, desired features, AI acceptance, privacy expectations, and usage preferences. It offers a reasonable basis for initial functional and non-functional requirements. However, the limited sample, incomplete documentation of response scales, and lack of quantitative details reduce the reliability and traceability of the results.

## Follow-up Actions

* Correct language and document-path inconsistencies.
* Document all response options and scales.
* Add exact response counts or percentages to the analysis.
* Add captions and references for the result images.
* Clarify consent, anonymity, and data handling.
* Validate the findings with a broader and more diverse student sample.
* Activate the history section.
