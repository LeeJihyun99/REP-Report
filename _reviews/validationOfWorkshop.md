## Review Scope

This review covers the team workshop conducted for the AI-assisted mental health application. The goal is to verify that the workshop process, participants, generated ideas, and feature priorities are documented clearly and can support requirements elicitation.

## Reviewed Artefacts

* Team Workshop
* Workshop Miro-board documentation

## Review Criteria

1. Correct artefact type and unique acronym
2. Clear workshop objective and method
3. Complete participant information
4. Traceable workshop results
5. Clear connection between ideas and priorities
6. Appropriate handling of safety-sensitive ideas
7. Sufficient information for deriving requirements

## Issues Identified

1. The workshop date does not use the ISO date format used by the other artefacts.
2. The participant-approach description contains grammatical and spacing errors.
3. The metadata states that two participants attended, while the description repeatedly refers to one participant.
4. Two participants are not sufficient to represent the entire reference group of 180 students.
5. The connection between the generated ideas and the final list of 12 prioritized features is not fully documented.
6. The proposal that the AI should diagnose users requires validation by a Domain Expert.
7. Automatically initiating emergency support requires clarification regarding consent, privacy, feasibility, and legal responsibility.
8. The artefact does not document participant consent or the handling of sensitive mental-health information.

## Review Result

The workshop provides useful ideas and an initial prioritization of features. The combination of brainstorming, brainstorming paradox, and prioritization is suitable for requirements elicitation. However, inconsistencies in participant documentation, limited representativeness, and safety-sensitive feature proposals require further clarification and validation.

## Follow-up Actions

* Correct the date format and language errors.
* Clarify whether one or two participants took part.
* Document how the final feature list was derived.
* Validate the priorities with additional students.
* Review diagnostic and emergency-support ideas with the Domain Expert and Data Protection Officer.
* Document participant consent and data handling.
