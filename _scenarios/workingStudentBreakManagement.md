## $main Sarah manages her tight schedule and receives an automated study break prompt

Sarah Meyer is sitting at her desk at home in Cologne during the peak exam period, balancing her 20-ECTS coursework with her 16-hour work-week at an IT consultancy. Her digital calendar is completely packed, and she is experiencing chronic fatigue. She opens her laptop to resume writing code for a project submission while skipping meals. The intelligent, calendar-integrated wellness tool automatically detects her high-density workload period and recognizes her extended continuous sitting time. The system triggers an automated prompt for a mandatory short study break, presenting a quick micro-check-in that fits seamlessly into her schedule without creating cognitive overhead. Sarah accepts the prompt, and the application guides her through a quick grounding exercise before she returns refreshed to her work.

## $alternative Sarah postpones the break due to an imminent deadline

Sarah is working diligently on her project submission when the system triggers the automated prompt for a mandatory short study break due to extended sitting. However, an urgent work-related deadline is approaching in the next twenty minutes. Sarah uses the interactive interface to snooze the break reminder for a short interval. The system acknowledges the request, logs the postponement, and reschedules the break prompt to appear immediately after her urgent task window closes, ensuring she still takes the necessary recovery time later.

## $exception Sarah ignores repetitive wellness prompts during severe stress fatigue

Sarah reaches an extreme state of exhaustion during exam preparation and completely ignores the automated break prompts triggered by the wellness tool. Because she dismisses multiple successive notifications while pushing through her fatigue, the system detects her prolonged high-stress pattern. Recognizing that standard micro-check-ins are no longer sufficient, the application escalates its response by shifting into a low-stimulation UI mode and providing a direct, prominent option to access supportive counseling resources or relaxation guides.
