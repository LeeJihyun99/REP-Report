## $main David organizes specialized study circles for international exchange students

David Chen is hosting an online study circle as an active student coordinator in the International Student Buddy Program. He observes how language barriers and bureaucracy hinder international exchange students from building resilience during their first months. He utilizes the platform to set up anonymous exchange groups tailored specifically to international student topics like visa stress and German exam formats. International participants join the groups easily to share advice and build mutual encouragement networks early in the semester.

## $alternative David customizes group privacy settings for sensitive immigration discussions

While managing an international student discussion group, David notices that participants are hesitant to discuss sensitive visa renewal anxieties and bureaucratic hurdles openly, even within anonymous groups. David uses the platform's advanced group settings to enable end-to-end encrypted messaging and strict member-verification filters for that specific circle. This extra layer of security reassures the students, leading to a much more open and supportive discussion.

## $exception An inactive group becomes stagnant due to low student participation

David creates an international student study circle at the beginning of the semester, but due to conflicting lecture timetables and heavy initial coursework, none of the invited exchange students join or post messages for three weeks. The group remains completely stagnant. The system detects the inactivity threshold and sends David a notification suggesting either archiving the group or merging its members with another active buddy circle to foster better engagement.
