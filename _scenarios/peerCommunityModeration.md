## $main Julia moderates anonymous peer discussions to maintain a safe support space

Julia Becker is working from her study space as a Master's student and volunteer mentor in the university's peer-support network. She recognizes that students share their true feelings about academic stress and failure more openly with peers than with official counselors. She accesses the platform's anonymous peer-to-peer interaction space designed to overcome students' fear of social judgment. The system's automated moderation features flag and filter toxic behavior or misinformation in real-time. Julia successfully reviews flagged items and moderates discussions, ensuring the peer community space remains constructive, empathetic, and safe.

## $alternative An ambiguous student post requires human mentor intervention

A student submits an anonymous message to the peer community space that contains frustrated venting about exam difficulty. While it does not violate explicit hate speech filters, the automated system flags it as potentially distressing or borderline toxic. The post is routed to Julia's moderator dashboard for human review rather than being instantly published or deleted. Julia evaluates the context, adds a supportive resource link, and approves the post with a gentle advisory label.

## $exception A high volume of toxic messages overwhelms the automated moderation filter

During peak exam results week, a sudden wave of spam and aggressive messages floods the anonymous peer community space, temporarily exceeding the processing capacity of the automated moderation filter. Several inappropriate posts become visible to students before the system can catch them. Julia receives an urgent administrator alert, steps in manually to lock the affected discussion thread, and purges the violating content to restore safety to the platform.
