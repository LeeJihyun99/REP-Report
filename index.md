<h1>Requirements Engineering Document - Team D</h1>
  <h2>Our Product: AI-Assisted Mental Health Application</h2>

  <p>
    This Requirements Engineering document describes an AI-assisted mental
    health application designed to support university students experiencing
    academic stress, anxiety, burnout, loneliness, and related challenges.
  </p>

  <p>
    The application provides accessible, low-threshold support through daily
    emotional check-ins, personalized AI conversations, mood tracking,
    evidence-based wellness exercises, academic stress assistance, and
    connections to professional counseling or emergency services. It is
    intended to complement—not replace—qualified mental health professionals.
  </p>

  <p>
    The requirements were derived using surveys, interviews, workshops,
    literature research, scenarios, and stakeholder analysis. The project
    considers the perspectives of students, mental health professionals,
    developers, university administration, data protection experts, system
    maintainers, and regulators.
  </p>

  <h2>Document Overview</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>Project goals and system context</li>
    <li>Stakeholders and stakeholder roles</li>
    <li>Personas and usage scenarios</li>
    <li>Elicitation activities and results</li>
    <li>Functional and non-functional requirements</li>
    <li>Privacy, safety, and crisis-escalation considerations</li>
    <li>Reviews for validating the documented artefacts</li>
  </ul>

  <p>
    The overall objective is to define a safe, trustworthy, privacy-conscious,
    and student-centered system that offers immediate support while guiding
    users toward professional help when necessary.
  </p>
</section>
