## Details

The interview was conducted in person at TH Köln Campus Gummersbach and lasted approximately 1 hour 30 minutes. The participant was a Media Informatik master's student at TH Köln, representing the primary stakeholder group of the proposed AI-assisted mental health application. One member of the project team(The author) facilitated the interview and documented the participant's responses directly on a shared Miro board. The interview questions were provided beforehand to give the participant an overview of the discussion topics.

Rather than producing a transcript, the Miro board was used to collaboratively organize the participant's experiences, challenges, coping strategies, and expectations regarding AI-supported mental health applications. The documented insights were then analyzed and later served as the foundation for a follow-up workshop with the same participant, allowing the discussion to transition from identifying problems to collaboratively exploring possible solutions.

The interview followed a semi-structured format based on a predefined questionnaire. The participant agreed that notes could be documented during the session. Instead of creating a transcript, the discussion points, ideas, and observations were recorded collaboratively on a Miro board, which was later analyzed to identify user needs and derive system requirements.


### The interview resulted in:

* Completed interview questionnaire
* Collaborative Miro board containing discussion notes and key insights
* Qualitative analysis of user needs and pain points
* Requirement candidates derived from the interview findings


## Goals

The primary objective of the interview was to gain a deeper understanding of students' mental health challenges and their expectations regarding an AI-assisted mental health support application. Unlike the quantitative survey, the interview aimed to uncover the motivations behind students' experiences and identify underlying user needs.

The interview had the following goals:

* Understand students' sources of academic stress and emotional challenges.
* Explore the underlying reasons behind students' stress and current coping strategies.
* Identify unmet needs that are not easily captured through quantitative surveys.
* Explore students' expectations and concerns regarding AI-assisted mental health support.
* Understand the limitations of existing AI and mental health applications from a user's perspective.
* Derive functional and non-functional requirement candidates for the proposed system.
* Provide qualitative insights that would guide the subsequent workshop and support collaborative feature elicitation.

## Results

The interview provided qualitative insights into the challenges and expectations of a university student regarding AI-assisted mental health support. Beyond identifying common academic stressors, the interview revealed underlying issues such as long-term career-related anxiety, perfectionism, and low self-confidence. The participant emphasized the need for personalized, evidence-based support rather than generic AI advice, while also expressing the importance of maintaining professional counseling as a complementary resource. These findings complemented the survey results by explaining the motivations behind users' needs and served as the foundation for the subsequent workshop and the derivation of functional and non-functional requirements.
