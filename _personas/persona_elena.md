## Description

Dr. Elena Voss is 45 years old and has been working as a senior psychologist at TH Köln's Student Counseling Center (Zentrale Studienberatung) for over eight years. She holds a PhD in Clinical Psychology and specializes in youth academic anxiety.

Dr. Voss and her team are chronically overbooked, especially during January-February and June-July exam periods. Students often wait up to four weeks for an initial consultation appointment, by which time minor academic distress has often escalated into severe clinical depression or crisis states.

Her primary goal is to promote preventative self-help tools that empower students to manage stress early on their own. She advocates for a digital system that provides low-barrier, self-guided coping strategies while offering a reliable, direct escalation mechanism that connects high-risk students immediately to emergency hotline resources or her clinic's priority queue.
