## Description

Aisha Khan is 21 years old and an exchange student from India, currently in her third semester of Media Informatics at TH Köln. This is her first time living outside her home country, and the transition to independent living in Cologne has been challenging.

Aisha struggles with the German university system's emphasis on independent self-study, which differs significantly from the structured guidance she was used to back home. She frequently feels isolated and finds it difficult to connect with local peers outside of lectures. When academic deadlines accumulate, her stress manifests physically through insomnia and lack of focus.

She hesitates to seek formal psychological counseling due to cultural taboos surrounding mental health. Aisha benefits greatly from peer-to-peer exchange and micro-interventions like short, guided mindfulness breaks. An anonymous student community feature would allow her to share her experiences and realize she is not alone in her struggles.
