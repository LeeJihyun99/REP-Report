## Description

David Chen is 23 years old, in his fifth Bachelor semester, and serves as an active student coordinator in the university's International Student Buddy Program. 

David frequently organizes welcome events and weekly study circles for international exchange students. He observes firsthand how language barriers, bureaucracy, and homesickness hinder international students from building personal resilience during their first months in Cologne.

David advocates for inclusive, culturally aware peer support. He wants the system to facilitate anonymous exchange groups specifically tailored to international student topics (e.g., visa stress, language anxiety, adapting to German exam formats), enabling students to build mutual encouragement networks early in the semester.
