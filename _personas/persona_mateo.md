## Description

Mateo Silva is 23 years old and moved from Brazil to Germany to pursue his Master's degree in Digital Sciences at TH Köln. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Rio de Janeiro. Since arriving in Germany six months ago, he has been adapting to an entirely new academic and cultural environment.

Mateo is highly motivated academically, but the combination of studying in a foreign language and navigating complex administrative structures at the university causes him severe anxiety. During exam periods, his stress levels peak due to tight deadlines and high self-imposed expectations. Because his family and close friends are in a different time zone, he often experiences acute isolation and homesickness when feeling overwhelmed.

He prefers low-barrier, mobile-first digital solutions to track his mental well-being discreetly. Because he fears institutional stigma, he values absolute data privacy and anonymity. Mateo needs proactive, simple guidance that helps him manage stress and access local academic coping resources without overwhelming him with complex UI navigation.
