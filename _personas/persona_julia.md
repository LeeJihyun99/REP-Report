## Description

Julia Becker is 25 years old and a Master's student in Digital Sciences. She volunteers as a student mentor in the university's peer-support network, assisting lower-semester students with exam preparation and academic orientation.

Julia knows from her own experience that students share their true feelings about academic stress and failure far more openly with fellow students than with professors or official university counselors. However, she notices that fear of social judgment and reputation loss often keeps students from reaching out openly.

Julia wants to leverage anonymous peer-to-peer interaction within the mental health platform. She needs moderation features that ensure the peer community space remains constructive, empathetic, and safe from toxic behavior or misinformation.
