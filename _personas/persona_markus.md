## Description

Markus Hoffmann is 38 years old and works as a mental health counselor and crisis intervention coordinator at the university's psychological service. He has ten years of professional experience in suicide prevention and acute psychological first aid.

Markus is supportive of digital health technologies but remains cautious regarding automated AI chatbots. He is deeply concerned about safety risks when automated tools fail to recognize severe crisis signals, such as acute self-harm thoughts or severe mental breakdowns.

Markus requires the Student Mental Health System to incorporate strict safety protocols. Specifically, whenever automated text analysis or student check-in scores cross critical safety thresholds, the system must reliably present a prominent, single-tap human escalation path to professional crisis hotlines and regional emergency services.
