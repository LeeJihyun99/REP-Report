## Description

Lukas Weber is 24 years old and completing his final Bachelor's semester in Computer Science. He is currently writing his thesis while completing his remaining compulsory modules. 

Lukas suffers from severe perfectionism, which frequently leads to intense procrastination. He routinely ignores early physical red flags of mental fatigue, pushing through nights of last-minute cramming fueled by energy drinks. This unhealthy pattern culminates in acute performance panic days before major exams or thesis submission deadlines.

Lukas avoids traditional mental health apps because he finds long journal entries or complex tracking routines tedious. He requires gamified, automated tracking features that catch his downward burnout spirals early and trigger clear, low-stimulation UI modes during high-stress states to keep him grounded.
