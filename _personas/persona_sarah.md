## Description

Sarah Meyer is 22 years old and in her fourth semester of Digital Sciences at TH Köln. She lives in Cologne and works 16 hours a week as a working student at an IT consultancy to fund her living expenses and tuition.

Balancing a demanding 20-ECTS course load with a working student job leaves Sarah with virtually no downtime. During the lecture period, her calendar is strictly tightly packed. When exam periods approach, the cumulative pressure leads to chronic fatigue, reduced productivity, and emotional exhaustion. She often skips meals and neglects self-care to meet project submission deadlines.

Sarah needs an intelligent, calendar-integrated wellness tool that automatically recognizes high-density workload periods. She requires quick, micro-check-ins and automated prompts for mandatory short study breaks that seamlessly fit into her tight daily calendar without causing additional cognitive overhead.
