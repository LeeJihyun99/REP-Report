## Profile

University students who seek accessible mental health support for challenges such as stress, burnout, anxiety, exam-related pressure, or difficulties adapting to university life. This stakeholder group also includes students acting as peer supporters who provide informal academic and emotional support to fellow students.


## Areas of activity

* Register and manage an account
* Complete daily mood check-ins
* Chat with the AI assistant
* Track emotional progress
* Receive personalized coping strategies and reminders
* Access emergency mental health resources
* Provide informal academic and emotional peer support
* Guide fellow students toward appropriate university support services


## Relevance as a Stakeholder

Students are the primary users of the application and include both those seeking support and those assisting their peers. Their needs, experiences, and feedback directly influence the system requirements, user experience, and future development of the application. Their satisfaction and willingness to use the system are essential to achieving the project’s goal of improving student well-being.

## Different types of Students
### Domestic Student
Local students who balance academic workloads with employment, personal responsibilities, and increased pressure during examination periods.
### International Student	
Students studying outside their home country who may face cultural adjustment, language barriers, unfamiliar institutional structures, and challenges in establishing social support networks.
### Peer Supporter	
Senior students, mentors, or student buddies who provide informal academic guidance and emotional support to fellow students. They may also use the application to identify appropriate coping resources, recognize situations requiring professional assistance, and direct students toward relevant university or emergency support services.
