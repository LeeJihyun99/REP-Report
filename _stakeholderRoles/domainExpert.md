## Profile

The Domain Expert is a licensed mental health professional, such as a university counselor, psychologist, or therapist. This stakeholder provides expert knowledge about psychological well-being, student mental health, evidence-based interventions, counseling workflows, and referral procedures. The Domain Expert validates that the application's recommendations are accurate, safe, ethical, and aligned with professional mental health practices.

## Typical Tasks

* Review AI-generated recommendations for psychological accuracy.
* Validate evidence-based coping strategies and mental health content.
* Identify inappropriate or potentially harmful AI responses.
* Advise on student counseling workflows and professional care procedures.
* Define when users should be referred to professional or emergency support.
* Verify information about university counseling services.
* Recommend improvements to ensure safe and ethical system behavior.

## relationshipToProject: 
The University Mental Health Counselor performs the Domain Expert role in the project. The counselor validates the clinical accuracy and safety of the application's mental health content, advises the development team on counseling and referral procedures, and helps connect AI-assisted support with professional university counseling services.
