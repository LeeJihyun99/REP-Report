## Profile

The Domain Expert is a mental health professional who provides expert knowledge about psychological well-being, student mental health, and evidence-based interventions. This stakeholder validates that the application's recommendations are accurate, safe, ethical, and aligned with professional mental health practices.

## Typical Tasks

* Review AI-generated recommendations for psychological accuracy.
* Validate evidence-based coping strategies and mental health content.
* Identify inappropriate or potentially harmful AI responses.
* Advise on best practices in student mental health support.
* Recommend improvements to ensure safe and ethical system behavior.
