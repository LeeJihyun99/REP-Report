## Profile

The User is a university student who uses the application to manage stress, anxiety, burnout, and other mental health challenges. Users interact with the AI chatbot, complete daily check-ins, monitor their emotional well-being, and receive personalized recommendations to support their mental health and academic success.

## Typical Tasks

* Complete daily mental health check-ins.
* Interact with the AI chatbot for mental health support.
* Track mood, stress levels, and personal well-being.
* Access personalized coping strategies and mental health resources.
* Manage personal settings, notifications, and privacy preferences.
* Contact professional support when recommended by the system.
