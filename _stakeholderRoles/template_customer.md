## Profile

The Customer / Project Sponsor represents the university or organization that initiates, funds, and oversees the development of the AI-assisted mental health application. This stakeholder ensures that the system aligns with organizational goals, addresses students' mental health needs, and provides value to the institution.

## Typical Tasks

* Define project objectives and success criteria.
* Provide funding and strategic direction for the project.
* Review project progress and prioritize business requirements.
* Evaluate whether the system meets organizational and stakeholder expectations.
* Approve major releases and deployment of the application.
* Assess the impact of the application on student well-being and university services.
