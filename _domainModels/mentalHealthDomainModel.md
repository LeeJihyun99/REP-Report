## Explanation

This domain model provides a structured overview of the key concepts, entities, and relationships relevant to the AI-driven mental health support and student well-being platform. It integrates central terms ranging from user profiles, emotional states, and tracking mechanisms to academic schedules, automated recommendations, and multi-tier emergency support interventions.
