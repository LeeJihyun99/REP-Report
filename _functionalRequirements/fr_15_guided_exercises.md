## Reasoning

The survey, workshop, and literature review consistently highlighted the importance of meditation, mindfulness, and scientifically validated coping techniques. Participants preferred personalized mental wellness exercises rather than only a breathing exercise. Expanding this feature allows the system to better support different stress situations while differentiating the application from existing mental health apps.
